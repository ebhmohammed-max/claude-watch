# Rain episode — visual shot register

Working register for *What Happened When It Rained for Days Before Houses?*
Status: **drafts only.** Nothing here is timed to narration, and no shot is
final. All ancient scenes are AI-generated illustrative reconstructions, not
photographs of real sites or artifacts.

Legend: **A** = usable draft · **C** = usable with caveats · **R** = replace
or drop

## Recurring problems

- **Posed people fail** (shorts-like wraps, bare backs, staged groups).
  Distant silhouettes and rear views work; close posed figures do not.
- **Shelters default to neat A-frame or cone tents**, which overstates what is
  known. Cap at one such shot per section, captioned as illustrative.
- **Studio-style object shots fail** (knapped tools, bedding heaps). Objects
  work when they sit in natural ground scatter.
- **Warm sunset grades** creep in and clash with the overcast set.
